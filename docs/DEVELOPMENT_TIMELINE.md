# Development Timeline

LIBA progressed from a smaller proof-of-concept setup to a larger integrated mobile platform with a robotic arm.

## Early prototype
The early setup focused on validating basic mechanical movement, arm articulation, electronics, and control concepts.

## Integration and testing
The project moved toward integration of the mobile chassis, multi-joint arm, power system, sensors, and embedded control. Photos document wiring and physical testing.

## Larger prototype
The later prototype combines the mobile base and arm in a more complete configuration, with testing on a marked navigation surface.

**Photo labels are intentionally neutral:** individual images have not yet been verified against a specific date or phase.
